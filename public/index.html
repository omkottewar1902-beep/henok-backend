Three steps, once

Stick it. Forget it.
It works when you need it.

Setup takes about two minutes in the app. After that the sticker does the work on its own.

1Stick

Stick the QR on the item

Peel and place it on the windshield, the collar, the bag handle. Then activate it in the app and add up to three emergency contacts.

2Scan

A finder scans it

No app needed on their side. Any phone camera opens the scan page, and it loads in every country on any network.

3Call

Their dialer opens

One tap connects them to you on a routed line. If you can't answer, the call falls through to your contacts. Your number stays private the whole time.

Four kinds of QR

The same sticker, wherever it matters

Each QR carries the details that fit the item — plate number, pet name, bag description — so the finder knows what they're holding.

Car

Someone clips your bumper in a parking garage and can't find you. They scan the sticker on your windshield and you're talking in ten seconds.

Carries your plate number, so a tow crew or a first responder knows the vehicle is the right one.

Pet

What's in the app

Reachable when it counts.
Quiet the rest of the time.

Masked calls

Their dialer opens on a routed line. Your real number never reaches the browser, their screen, or their call log.

Real-time scan alerts

The moment your QR is scanned, your phone knows — with the time, the place, and the device that did it.

Location-aware scan log

Every scan is saved with a map pin, so you can see where your item turned up and when.

Multi-contact fallback

If you don't pick up, the finder can reach up to three people you trust, in the order you set.

Blocked-caller list

One tap blocks a number for good. They can still scan; the call simply never connects.

Printable PDF sticker

Lost the original? Download the same sticker from the app and print a replacement in a minute.
