QR stickers that put strangers in touch with owners — without exposing phone numbers.

Jcscan2connect gives you an emergency QR sticker for your car, pet, luggage, or valuables. When a finder scans it, they can reach you (or the people you trust) with a single tap. Real numbers are never revealed.

How it works

Three steps. Owner and finder both stay private.

1

Stick it

Peel and apply the QR sticker to your car, pet tag, luggage, laptop, or bike. Each sticker is unique to your account.

2

Scan

A finder points any phone camera at the sticker. A safe web page opens — no download needed.

3

Call

They tap "Call Owner" and their native dialer opens. Your registered contacts stay in the loop, phone numbers never travel through the browser.

Alerts to the people who matter

You register up to three contacts when you activate a QR. When your sticker is scanned, we can notify those contacts by SMS so they know a scan happened, even if the finder never taps "Call". Contacts opt in themselves through a double-opt-in confirmation before they receive any alerts.

Read the SMS opt-in details

Message flow

  1. Owner adds a contact's number in the app with a digital consent checkbox.
  2. Contact receives one confirmation SMS.
  3. Contact replies YES to opt in — or the number is deleted after 24 h.
  4. From then on, contact receives an alert only when the QR is scanned. Reply STOP anytime to opt out.

What it's for

Sticker use cases we've built for.

🚗 Cars

Blocked parking, minor bumps, lights left on, or an accident. A finder can reach you in seconds without leaving a note.

🐕 Pets

Slip the QR onto a collar tag. If your pet is found, the finder is one tap away from you and your family.

💼 Luggage & valuables

Bags, laptops, bikes, keys. Turn "found something" into "returned to owner" without exposing your number publicly.
