Privacy Policy

Jcscan2connect — Emergency QR alert service

Last updated: September 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Jcscan2connect ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and safeguards your information when you use our mobile application, our website, and our emergency QR alert service (collectively, the "Service"). Jcscan2connect is a sole proprietorship registered at 2220 Fraser St, Aurora, CO 80014, United States.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information: full name, email address, mobile number, and address you provide during signup.
  • QR profile information: the type of item you protect (vehicle, pet, luggage, other), item identifiers (e.g. vehicle number, pet name), and up to three emergency contacts (name, relationship, mobile number) that you add.
  • Scan events: when someone scans your QR code, we log approximate location (if the scanner permits), IP address, user agent, and timestamp so we can show you a scan history and detect abuse.
  • Payment information: processed by Stripe. We do not store card numbers.

2. How we use information

  • To operate the Service — routing calls, sending emergency SMS alerts, showing scan history.
  • To notify you about scan events, incoming calls, and account activity.
  • To bill for QR activations via Stripe.
  • To secure the Service (rate limiting, fraud detection).
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. SMS messaging — A2P 10DLC disclosure

We use Twilio to deliver SMS alerts. When someone scans your QR code, we may send an SMS to your registered emergency contacts letting them know a scan event occurred. Message frequency is scan-driven; typically 0–2 messages per month per contact.

Emergency contacts are added by the account owner during QR setup with the contact's prior verbal consent to be reached in emergency scan events. Contacts can reply STOP at any time to opt out of further messages, or reply HELP for assistance. Message and data rates may apply.

We do not share mobile numbers or SMS opt-in data with third parties or affiliates for their marketing purposes. No mobile information is sold or rented under any circumstances.

4. How we share information

We share personal information only with:

  • Twilio — to send SMS alerts and route calls with the caller's real number masked.
  • Stripe — to process payments.
  • Supabase — our database host in the United States.
  • Law enforcement — only when compelled by valid legal process.

We never sell personal information.

5. Data retention

Account and QR data are retained for the life of your account. Scan and call logs are retained for 24 months for security and audit purposes. You may request deletion at any time by emailing support@jcscan2connect.com.

6. Your rights

  • Access, correct, or delete your account information from within the app.
  • Disable a QR code at any time so it stops working when scanned.
  • Remove an emergency contact from a QR at any time.
  • Request full account deletion via support@jcscan2connect.com.

7. Children's privacy

The Service is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

8. Security

We use industry-standard measures including TLS for data in transit, hashed authentication credentials, and access controls. No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be announced in the app and reflected in the "Last updated" date above.

10. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email support@jcscan2connect.com or write to Jcscan2connect, 2220 Fraser St, Aurora, CO 80014, USA.
